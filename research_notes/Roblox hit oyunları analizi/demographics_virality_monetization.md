# Roblox Player Demographics, Discovery/Virality Mechanics and Monetization Patterns (cross-era, to Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-08. Main primary sources: Roblox Q4 2025 and Q3 2026 shareholder letters (SEC 8-K exhibits), Roblox Creator Docs (Discovery page last updated 2026-11-06), the Roblox newsroom (Apr 2025), and the Roblox 10-K/10-Q filings. Many game-level figures come from trackers and blogs (RoWatcher, profitable.app, RoLearn); each such figure is flagged as secondary or third-party. I could not find any Naavik or Deconstructor of Fun pieces through search, so analyst views here come from other outlets.

## Q1: What is the current (2025-2026) age breakdown of Roblox users, and DAU by region?

### Takeaway
Roblox has moved toward older players. Among users who had passed an age check in Q3 2026, 34% were under 13, 37% were 13-17 and 29% were 18+. In the US, 37% were 18+. Total DAU fell for the first time ever, to 138M in Q3 2026 (-9% YoY). Outlets link the drop to the mandatory age-check and chat-restriction rollout. Growth is now concentrated in APAC and emerging markets (India, Japan, Saudi Arabia), while US/Canada shrinks.

### Cited Findings
**Age, across eras**
- 2024 full-year age split as reported by an aggregator: under 9: 20%; 9-12: 20%; 13-16: 16%; 17-24: 25%; 25+: 19%. That is roughly 40% under 13 and 60% 13+. — [DemandSage (aggregator of Roblox disclosures)](https://www.demandsage.com/how-many-people-play-roblox/)
- Q2 2025: Newzoo reported 13+ user growth of 54% vs 22% for under-13s. 13+ users accounted for about 66% of hours. — [Newzoo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)
- Q2 2025: 39.7M under-13 DAU, up from 32.4M a year earlier. — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1192573/daily-active-users-roblox/)
- Q3 2025: 151M DAU. About two-thirds of DAU were 13+, and 13+ users grew around 89% YoY against around 44% for under-13s. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/?p=309290). Newzoo's under-13 growth figure for Q2 differs from this; the periods also differ.
- Q4 2025: 144M DAU (+69% YoY) and 35B hours (+88% YoY). Roblox did not report Q4 2025 age demographics because of the age-check transition. As of Jan 31, 2026, 45% of DAU had age-checked; of those, 35% were under 13, 38% were 13-17 and 27% were 18+. — [Roblox Q4 2025 Shareholder Letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)
- Q3 2026: among age-checked DAU in the week ended Sep 30, 2026, 34% were under 13, 37% were 13-17 and 29% were 18+, up from 27% 18+ in Q2. In the US, 18+ rose to 37% of age-checked DAU from 33% in Q2. US 18-34 DAU grew +26% YoY (hours +23%), and US 18+ DAU grew +18% YoY. — [Roblox Q3 2026 Shareholder Letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)
- Age-check coverage at the end of Q3 2026: 64% of global DAU, including more than 80% of 13-17 DAU. By market: US 75%, UK 75%, Australia 80%. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)

**Totals and region**
- Q3 2026: 138M DAU (-9% YoY, +12% QoQ, +55% over two years). Hours were nearly 34B (-15% YoY). — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm). GG Recon calls this "the first annual fall in the company's history" and attributes it mainly to age checks and chat restrictions for unchecked accounts. The stock fell about 16% the next day. — [GG Recon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/roblox-daily-players-fall-q3-2026/)
- Q3 2026 regional DAU as reported by a third party: US & Canada 24M (-10%), Europe 32M (-5%), APAC 42M (-9%), Rest of World 40M (-11%). These figures sum to 138M. **Unverified:** the official supplemental PDF presents them only as image charts, and I could not extract them. — [GamingAmigos](https://www.gamingamigos.com/post/roblox-q3-2026), [Securities.io](https://www.securities.io/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion/)
- Q3 2026 country growth, company-reported (DAU YoY): India +37%, Japan +33%, Saudi Arabia +30%, Germany +13%, France +12%. Hours grew +28% in India and +22% in Japan. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)
- Q4 2025: US & Canada DAU grew +32% YoY and DAU outside US & Canada grew +79%. APAC had the strongest hours growth. — [Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)
- Age-check rollout: mandatory for chat in late Q4 2025 in Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands, and global by early Jan 2026. Management guided to a mid-single-digit headwind to engagement growth and a low-single-digit headwind to bookings growth. — [Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)

### Inferences
- The age-checked mix (about one-third each under 13, 13-17 and 18+) probably over-represents teens, since more than 80% of 13-17s have checked against 64% overall. The true under-13 share of all DAU may be higher than 34%.
- US/Canada makes up only about 17% of DAU (if the third-party regional split is right) but monetizes far better per user than other regions. APAC and Rest of World together are about 60% of DAU. That spread is why regional pricing matters (see Q3).
- Mapping age to genres: I found no Roblox-published genre-by-age table. Indirect evidence points one way. US 18-34 growth sits alongside the rise of shooters and competitive games (RIVALS is named as a growing tenured title), while the 2025 mega-hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot) are idle/collection loops with meme IP that travelled through TikTok and YouTube to young audiences. This mapping is an inference, not a sourced fact.

### Gaps
- Official absolute regional DAU for Q3 2026 (in the supplemental charts, which I could not extract).
- The pre-2024 under-13 share series (for example, 2020-2022 under-13 shares of about 50% or more). I did not verify it in this session.
- An official genre × age engagement breakdown. Creator Analytics lets developers break KPIs down by age group, but no aggregate table is published.

## Q2: How does Roblox's recommendation algorithm decide what gets promoted? What role do creators, memes, live events and updates play?

### Takeaway
Home "Recommended for You" (RFY) runs in two stages, retrieval then ranking. Ranking scores only players who arrived organically through RFY, using per-user averages: play-through rate from the impression, first-play bounce (under 60 s and 61-180 s), play days per user (D1, D2-7, D8-28), playtime capped at 60 minutes per user per game per day, co-play, and spend days and Robux per user. A game is tested on new cohorts ("explore") and pushed to similar cohorts if they engage and spend ("expand"). Viral spikes come from outside the algorithm: TikTok/YouTube memes, "admin abuse" live events and cross-game stunts. Ranking metrics then decide whether that spike becomes durable recommended traffic.

### Cited Findings
- Retrieval picks candidate games using engagement, retention and monetization, and "even a small number of players" can be enough for a game to be considered. Ranking uses signals only from users acquired through RFY; players from ads, curation, friends, search or social are excluded. — [Roblox Creator Docs: Discovery (updated 2026-11-06)](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- The "most important" signals are play-through rate from the sort, first-play bounce rate (under 60 s and 61-180 s buckets), play days per user (D1, D2-7, D8-28), and playtime per user capped at 60 min per user per game per day. "Important" signals are intentional co-play days (joins, invites, private servers), qualified play sessions, spend days per user and Robux spent per user. All are per-user averages, so small games are not penalized for size. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- Deprioritized: metadata that leads with monetary rewards, thumbnails or metadata that do not match gameplay, near-copies of existing games or visuals, and games with a default thumbnail (not eligible for Home impressions). Benchmark games do not affect ranking. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery); also [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- Impressions also depend on seasonality: weekly peaks on Saturdays, plus summer, back-to-school and holiday patterns. Other surfaces are Continue Playing, Friends, Sponsored (ads), Curated sorts, hand-curated Standout Games, Live Events, Search (semantic), Discover/top charts and Notifications. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- 2025 Discovery Roadmap: RFY was re-optimized for long-term retention and organic recommendations. Discovery served more than 10.5B impressions per day, up from 7.5B a year earlier. Roblox says it targets per-user engagement, not total engagement. QPTR (qualified play-through rate) can dip temporarily as impressions grow. — [DevForum 2025 Discovery Roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox’s-2025-discovery-roadmap-and-ways-to-reach-and-engage-your-audience/3557533); [Tubefilter, Mar 20 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/03/20/roblox-discovery-roadmap-2025/)
- In Q4 2025, RFY changes drove a double-digit rise in unique experiences surfaced per 1M recommendations. Users played more than 24 unique experiences per month in 2025, up double digits YoY. Outside the top 10, engagement grew +68% and Robux spend +53% in Q4 2025. — [Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)
- In Q3 2026, RFY was refined to use age-cohort data. About 40% of the weekly RFY top 100 games by impressions were created in the prior three months. Two AI-built games ("Break and Steal an Egg" and "Huss Valley") reached the top 20 by CCU. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)
- Steal a Brainrot (SpyderSammy, owned by DoBig Studios; released May 16, 2025) spread through TikTok and YouTube clips of stealing, strategy tips and kids' reactions to losing Brainrots. It uses Italian-brainrot meme characters. CCU history: about 5M in July 2025; 20M in August (passing Fortnite's 15.3M record); 24M at the Sep 13 "Extinct Event"; 25.4M in October 2025; average CCU of about 1M in September 2025. A Bruno Mars in-game concert in Jan 2026 peaked at 12.8M CCU. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot (citing IGN, PocketGamer.biz, Variety, etc.)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot). Roblox's own figure is a record 25M CCU in September 2025. — [Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)
- A staged "admin war" between the Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot developers on Aug 23, 2025 pushed both games to records; Steal a Brainrot became the second Roblox game past 20M CCU. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot). Grow a Garden peaked at about 21-22M CCU in mid-2025, and the platform peaked at 47.4M concurrent players. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/mmo/with-21-million-concurrent-players-roblox-hit-grow-a-garden-is-bigger-than-half-of-steam-combined-but-players-are-already-flooding-another-far-weirder-competitor)
- "Admin abuse" is a scheduled event (Grow a Garden held them weekly, on Saturdays, around update drops) in which the developer joins servers and hands out rare items or weather effects. Beebom argues these events pulled more than 20M players but push developers to chase spikes at the expense of core systems, which "hurts them in the long run." — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/admin-abuse-might-save-dying-roblox-games-but-hurts-them-in-the-long-run); [DigitalCitizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/grow-a-garden-admin-abuse-schedule/)

### Inferences
- A virality playbook can be read off the signals. Short-video memes and live events produce off-platform demand, which RFY does not score directly. When those visitors play, RFY explores the game with similar cohorts, and the game survives only if those organic cohorts show low bounce, repeat days (D2-7, D8-28) and spend days.
- The 60-minute daily playtime cap means multi-day return behavior is worth more than marathon sessions. That favors idle and "come back to collect" loops (offline income, crop timers, pet aging) over long one-sitting sessions.
- Saturday events line up with Roblox's weekly traffic peak, which increases their effect.
- Thumbnail and icon CTR shows up as "play-through rate after seeing the game in the sort." Misleading thumbnails are explicitly deprioritized, so a clickbait CTR gain can backfire through the bounce signal.

### Gaps
- Signal weights and numeric thresholds are not published.
- I found no official D1/D7 benchmark values. They appear only inside each developer's dashboard.
- I found no primary data on TikTok/YouTube view-to-CCU conversion.

## Q3: What are benchmark payer conversion rates and revenue per CCU for Roblox games? How do monetization mechanics work?

### Takeaway
Roblox publishes definitions and in-dashboard benchmarks for these metrics, but no public conversion or ARPDAU numbers. Platform-level, Roblox had about 30M monthly unique payers against 138M DAU in Q3 2026; bookings were $1.70B (-12% YoY) and DevEx payouts $403M. Game-level figures come from third-party estimates: roughly 1-3% payer conversion, and about $0.003-0.006 net ARPDAU for conservatively monetized games. These figures are low-confidence. Creator earnings now come from passes, developer products, subscriptions and Creator Rewards; Creator Rewards replaced Premium engagement-based payouts on Jul 24, 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Platform economics**
- Q3 2026: revenue $1,572M (+16%); bookings $1,697M (-12% YoY); monthly unique payers 30M (-16% YoY); bookings per payer +5% YoY; bookings per hour +4% globally and +2% in US/CA; DevEx fees $403M (24% of bookings). Q4 2026 bookings guidance is $1.89-2.00B (-15% to -10% YoY). — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)
- FY2025: bookings $6.79B (+55%) and revenue $4.89B (+36%). Q4 2025: bookings $2.22B (+63%) and 36.7M monthly unique payers (about 2x YoY). FY2025 DevEx was $1.503B against $922.8M in FY2024. The top 1,000 creators averaged $1.3M each in 2025 (+50% or more). — [Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)
- The DevEx rate rose 8.5% effective Sep 5, 2025, to about $0.0038 per Robux (100k Robux ≈ $380). Engagement-based payouts (EBP, based on Premium subscribers' hours) ended Jul 23, 2025, and were replaced on Jul 24, 2025, by Creator Rewards, which pays on metrics tied to engagement and retention. — [Roblox 10-Q Q3 2025 (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000327/rblx-20250930.htm); [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm); [Business Wire, Sep 5 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250905587466/en)
- Grow a Garden led Roblox in May 2025 with about $12M in revenue and 1.2B hours, according to secondary reporting. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-top-earning-games-in-june-2025/) (secondary; treat as an estimate)

**Game-level KPIs and benchmarks**
- Roblox defines conversion as the share of DAU who pay, and ARPDAU as conversion × ARPPU. Its dashboard benchmarks can be broken down by age group, country, language, platform and OS. — [Creator Docs: Monetization analytics](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963)
- Developers report that payer conversion and ARPDAU fall as a game gains popularity, because broader and less committed cohorts arrive. — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/payer-conversion-rate-and-arpdau-decreasing-as-game-gains-more-popularity/3898921)
- Third-party estimates: typical conversion of 1-3%, and a well-monetized game earning 3-8 Robux per visit. — [profitable.app calculator](https://profitable.app/tools/roblox-revenue-calculator). Net ARPDAU of about $0.003-0.006 for conservative monetization. — [RoWatcher, DevEx Math 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/devex-math-in-2026-what-you-actually-take-home-per-1-000-players). These are low-confidence.
- Anecdotal revenue per CCU: a 2k+ CCU game made about $3,800 per month ([SozAI transcript of developer video](https://sozai.app/transcript/2k-ccu-roblox-game-earnings/)). A forum example put 5k CCU at about 120k Robux per day, or about $60k per year. A 100 CCU game makes about $190-570 per year ([RoWatcher income ladder](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn)). About 85% of developers earn under $100 per month, according to the same third-party source. These figures are anecdotal and vary widely.

**Pricing tools**
- Regional pricing for passes launched Apr 2025, with Roblox setting regional prices automatically from a default price. Early tests raised the share of users spending by about 17% in Mexico, 26% in Brazil and 52% in the Philippines, with a 26% median across tested countries. Price Optimization tests produced a 4% median earnings increase; Slap Battles gained more than 15%. Price Optimization eligibility is 60k or more transactions per month. — [Roblox Newsroom, Apr 22 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-launches-regional-pricing-for-in-experience-items)
- Both tools are now combined into "Managed Pricing," which covers passes and developer products. Prices must be scripted rather than hard-coded, and regional prices interact with trading and gifting (GetUsersPriceLevelsAsync). — [Creator Docs: Managed pricing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/managed-pricing); [Price optimization](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/price-optimization.md)

**Mechanics in 2025 hits**
- Steal a Brainrot: Brainrots on a conveyor belt generate income over time; rarer units cost more; players can steal from others' bases, with a temporary shield; traps and items are sold for currency or Robux; rebirth resets progress for multipliers. Polygon criticized it as pay-to-win because the best items are cash-only. Automation scripts and cheating were widespread. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Grow a Garden: buy seeds, harvest crops for money, with weather and mutation events and limited-time event items during admin abuse. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/mmo/with-21-million-concurrent-players-roblox-hit-grow-a-garden-is-bigger-than-half-of-steam-combined-but-players-are-already-flooding-another-far-weirder-competitor); [DigitalCitizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/grow-a-garden-admin-abuse-schedule/)

### Inferences
- The Q3 2026 platform payer rate is about 30M monthly payers / 138M DAU, roughly 22% on a monthly-unique basis. That is not comparable to daily game-level conversion; daily per-game conversion of 1-3% is consistent with it.
- Bookings fell faster than DAU in Q3 2026 (-12% vs -9%). Payers fell faster still (-16%) while spend per payer rose (+5%). The paying base is concentrating.
- Spend days per user and Robux per user are ranking signals. Monetization therefore affects distribution as well as revenue: small, frequent purchases (developer products such as boosts, rerolls, rare seeds and shields) probably help more than a single large gamepass. This is an inference from the ranking spec.
- RNG rarity tiers combined with time-limited event items create FOMO and repeat-day visits. Stealing and trading systems give players a social reason to spend (protection, status).

### Gaps
- No official public payer-conversion or ARPDAU benchmarks; they exist only inside dashboards.
- No credible public revenue-per-CCU benchmark.
- No primary payout data for Creator Rewards (rates, share of creator earnings).
- No sourced data on whether Roblox-wide policy on paid random items (loot-box disclosure) changed in 2025-26. Not researched.

## Q4: What design traits do analysts and Roblox's docs cite for hits? What makes idle/passive-income games retain players?

### Takeaway
Roblox's own guidance amounts to building for organic per-user retention: low bounce in the first 60-180 seconds, repeat play days, co-play with friends, and accurate, unique metadata. Outside commentary adds simple, readable loops; meme IP that spreads on social video; collectible/RNG economies with trading; persistent progression; frequent updates and Saturday live events; and social conflict mechanics such as stealing. Idle/passive-income games keep players by giving them a reason to come back (accumulated cash, growing crops, aging pets). That matches the D2-7 and D8-28 play-day signals while the 60-minute cap limits the value of long sessions.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox: improving retention, engagement and monetization directly improves recommendations. Underperforming games keep getting retested with new cohorts, so iteration pays off. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery); [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- Intentional co-play (joins, invites, private servers) is an explicit "important" ranking signal. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- Steal a Brainrot's loop: passive income from owned units, rarity escalation, PvP theft, defensive shields and traps, and rebirth. TikTok and YouTube spread came from emotionally charged stealing moments. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Meme culture "can translate directly into Roblox game success," according to one outlet on the brainrot wave. — [RoWatcher: The Brainrot Invasion](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-brainrot-invasion-how-absurd-meme-games-conquered-roblox-s-charts) (secondary)
- Long-lived games share player-driven trading economies (leaving means losing a collection), a consistent update cadence, deep progression that takes weeks or months, social infrastructure (Discord, video communities) and committed developers. — [RoWatcher: Lifespan of a Hit](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-lifespan-of-a-hit-why-90-of-roblox-s-top-100-games-fade-within-a-year) (undated, no stated methodology)
- Adopt Me!: pets persist and age across sessions, eggs are released regularly, and there is a trading economy. Blox Fruits: a long grind to the level cap, and fruit rarity with community-assigned trade values. Brookhaven: open roleplay with no win or fail state; its audience may age out. — [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-games-fail); [creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/blog/best-roblox-games-to-study); [RoWatcher: Brookhaven](https://rowatcher.com/news/brookhaven-rp-at-80-billion-visits-inside-roblox-s-unstoppable-digital-dollhouse) (secondary blogs)
- Roblox named AdoptMe! and RIVALS as growing tenured experiences in Q4 2025, alongside new hits Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot and 99 Nights in the Forest. — [Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)
- Roblox Innovation Awards 2025: Grow a Garden won Best New Experience and Steal a Brainrot won Best Creative Direction. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://pocketgamer.biz/roblox-reveals-2025-innovation-awards-winners)

### Inferences
- Idle and passive income fit the algorithm well. Off-session accrual creates D1 and D2-7 return visits ("come back to collect"), sessions are short and low-friction for young players, and there are many small chances to spend on boosts, rare spawns or protection. Stealing adds loss aversion, which pulls players back to guard their base and gives them a reason to recruit friends (co-play).
- The age shift suggests two different design tracks. Young players respond to meme IP plus collection and idle loops; the growing 18-34 cohort responds to competitive and skill-based games (RIVALS-type). This is an inference without genre-by-age data.
- AI-assisted rapid production (Q3 2026) together with RFY's "explore" phase favoring new games (about 40% of the top 100 are under three months old) means trends get cloned and saturated within weeks. Being first to a meme format matters more than before.

### Gaps
- I could not access original Naavik, Deconstructor of Fun or Game Developer analyses through search. Their specific claims are not represented here.
- No primary RDC 2025/2026 talk content on hit design was retrieved.

## Q5: Why do viral games lose players, and how do the long-lived ones (Adopt Me, Brookhaven, Blox Fruits) retain them?

### Takeaway
Most hits decay fast. A third-party analysis estimates about 90% of top-100 entrants leave within a year, and only 1-5% of peak CCU remains by year two. The usual causes are novelty that runs out, no progression or economy, update stalls, pay-to-win backlash, exploits that damage the economy, and developer disputes or turnover. Grow a Garden fell from about 21M peak CCU (Jun 2025) to roughly 30-50k CCU by mid-2026. Long-lived titles retain through persistent collections and trading economies, social roleplay and a steady update cadence.

### Cited Findings
- Hit lifecycle: a viral peak in weeks 1-4, a plateau through month 3, then a 5-15% monthly decline as content goes stale. Around month 12 the game drops below lobby critical mass and the decline accelerates; by year two, 1-5% of peak CCU remains. Causes: update stalls (more than quality), single-novelty mechanics, no progression, no collectible economy, and developers treating a hit as a quick payday. — [RoWatcher: Lifespan of a Hit](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-lifespan-of-a-hit-why-90-of-roblox-s-top-100-games-fade-within-a-year) (no methodology published; treat as directional)
- Grow a Garden: all-time high of about 21.1M CCU in Jun 2025. A tracker showed about 31k CCU on Jul 30, 2026, with daily peaks of about 44-48k. Cited causes include pay-to-win changes, pet additions and developer changes. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/?p=1655); [Roonby, Jan 2026](https://roonby.com/2026/01/13/grow-a-garden-is-losing-players-is-it-finally-dead/); [profitable.app tracker](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/grow-a-garden). A Spanish-language report claiming 16.4M CCU in June 2026 conflicts with these and is unreconciled. — [games.gg ES](https://games.gg/es/news/roblox-grow-a-garden-alcanza-16-millones-de-ccu/)
- Garden Horizons (a related spin-off) dropped from more than 1M to about 23k CCU within two weeks of launch. Cited causes: developer disputes, an exploit-damaged economy, performance problems and delayed console support. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/garden-horizons-player-count-drop-what-happened-and-is-the-game-dead/)

### Inferences
- Meme-driven games use up both their content and their meme. When the meme fades, off-platform demand disappears, and RFY then exposes the game's organic D8-28 retention, which is often weak.
- Economies built on RNG rarity and limited-time items run into inflation and power creep. Exploits and duplication glitches speed this up and wipe out the value of collections, which is the main thing keeping players. This connects the Garden Horizons and Grow a Garden failure accounts.
- Durable games turn time spent into a persistent, tradable asset (pets, fruits, items) or into social identity (roleplay). Either way, quitting has a real cost.

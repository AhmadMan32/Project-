# Roblox Hit Games That Launched or Broke Out, 2018–2022

Scope note: About 21 search and fetch calls. The strongest sources are Roblox's own year-end recaps (2021 official post; 2022 recap via GamesBeat and PocketGamer.biz), Wikipedia, and GamesPot/PCGamesN. Much per-game CCU and visit data online comes from third-party trackers (RoWatcher, Rotrends, Rolearn, Bloxodes) or SEO/AI-style aggregator pages. These often conflict, and their "all-time peak" fields are unreliable for 2018–2022 because many trackers only began logging later. Example: RoWatcher lists Pet Simulator X's all-time peak as "2K" while an official award cites 1.48M. Tracker figures are flagged throughout. Fields with no source are listed under Gaps and were not invented.

## When did each game break out and what triggered it? (Catalog of 2018–2022 hits)

### Takeaway
The 2018–2022 hits fall into waves. Adopt Me! broke out with pets (summer 2019) and peaked during the pandemic (2020 to April 2021). Piggy (2020) and Doors and Rainbow Friends (2022) carried horror, spread by YouTubers. Brookhaven (2020–21) led roleplay. Blox Fruits, Shindo Life, All Star Tower Defense, King Legacy and Anime Fighters (2021) led anime games. BedWars (May 2021) brought Minecraft-style PvP. Pet Simulator X (2021–22) led simulator-collectors. Triggers named in sources were: major content updates such as new eggs and pets, YouTuber and streamer playthroughs, TikTok, trading economies, the pandemic platform surge, and pop-culture clones such as Squid Game in Sept–Oct 2021.

1. **Adopt Me!** (Uplift Games, formerly DreamCraft)
   - Launched July 13, 2017. Started as family roleplay; adoptable pets were added in summer 2019, which "sharply increased its popularity" — [Wikipedia: Adopt Me!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!)
   - Visits: 3B by Dec 2019; 10B by July 2020; about 20B by March 2021 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!)
   - CCU: 1,615,085 on Apr 11, 2020; 1,786,076 on Oct 10, 2020; 1.92M in April 2021 with the Ocean Egg update (Apr 17, 2021), beating the earlier 1.78M record — [GameSpot (Apr 2021)](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/a-roblox-game-about-adopting-critters-just-set-a-new-concurrent-player-record/1100-6490282/); [PC Gamer (2020)](https://pcgamer.com/this-roblox-game-about-adopting-pets-had-more-players-this-week-than-mount-and-blade-ii-bannerlord); [Uplift press release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/bastion-uk/pressreleases/developers-behind-record-breaking-roblox-game-adopt-me-launch-new-studio-uplift-games-3098731). The April 2020 Pet Rock event drew 680K players. Player counts "typically triple during major updates" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!)
   - An earlier milestone above 400K CCU was tied to a specific update — [Roblox Developer Medium](https://medium.com/roblox-developer/outstanding-achievement-developers-discuss-update-that-got-adopt-me-over-400k-concurrent-players-f3592d06a88)
   - Loop: hatch pets from eggs, raise them by meeting their needs to earn Bucks, then fuse 4 fully grown pets into a Neon and 4 Neons into a Mega-Neon, and trade. Some pets are Robux-only — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!)
   - Monetization: mainly microtransactions. Over $16M revenue in the 2020 era ([List of Roblox games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)); about US$60M per year at roughly 40 staff (later figure). Rare pets resold for up to US$100–300 off-platform, which fed a scam subculture of "trust trades". Trade licenses and trade history were added in Nov 2020 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!). Brand tie-ins: Scoob! (2020), Sing 2 (2021), Minions (2022) — same source.
   - Age: primary players are "young children", younger than the average Roblox player — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!)
   - Rankings: #1 grossing in 2021 and 2022; #3 most visited in 2021 — [Roblox 2021](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2022/01/year-roblox-2021-data); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/80671/roblox-recap-reveals-70-new-experiences-surpassed-1-billion-visits-in-2022/)

### Inferences
- In this window, a breakout usually came from a single large update, not from the launch. Adopt Me!'s 2019 pets update and its 2020–21 egg drops are the clearest examples. The pattern then shifted: 2021–22 hits such as BedWars, Doors, Evade and Race Clicker reached the charts within weeks of launch, which suggests the algorithm and content-creator feedback loop had sped up.
- Of the candidates, the strongest true 2018–2022 originals are Tower of Hell (2018), Piggy (2020), Brookhaven (2020), Pet Simulator X (2021), BedWars (2021), Funky Friday (2021), Doors (2022) and Rainbow Friends (2022). Adopt Me!, Arsenal, Murder Mystery 2, MeepCity, Royale High, Jailbreak and Bloxburg were older games that broke out again or held on.

### Gaps
- No reliable source found for 2018–2022 peak CCU of: Brookhaven (2021), Doors (2022), Piggy (2020), Rainbow Friends, BedWars, Tower of Hell, Shindo Life, ASTD, King Legacy, Da Hood. Third-party "all-time peak" trackers are clearly incomplete for this period.
- Not verified this session (from background knowledge only; needs checking): Bee Swarm Simulator launched about March 2018 by Onett; Shindo Life is RELL World's renamed "Shinobi Life 2" (2020–21); Da Hood (about 2019, hood-RP/PvP combat); Tower Defense Simulator (Paradoxum Games, about 2019); All Star Tower Defense (about 2020); King Legacy (formerly King Piece, about 2020); Berry Avenue (about 2021–22); Strongman Simulator and Clicker Simulator (2022 simulator wave); Mega Easy Obby. None of these dates or numbers were confirmed in this session.
- Press coverage from Polygon, Kotaku, Dexerto or Bloomberg on specific breakouts was not found in searches. The Doors and Rainbow Friends viral narratives rest on PCGamesN plus low-quality aggregators.
- The Pet Simulator X "1.48M in November" figure is ambiguous between Nov 2022 and Nov 2023.

## Which genres rose in this period?

### Takeaway
Roblox's own data shows that roleplay and life-sim led, followed by action (fighting, survival, murder mystery), simulators, obbies and tycoons. Anime games and horror "surged" in 2021, and simulator-collectors saw a 4x jump in engagement time. In 2022, viral horror (Doors, Evade) and clicker simulators (Race Clicker) added momentum.

### Cited Findings
- 2021 top genres: 1 Role-Playing, 2 Action (Fighting, Survival, Murder Mystery), 3 Simulator, 4 Platformer/Obby, 5 Tycoon. Life-sim engagement time doubled year over year; anime and horror "saw a surge"; simulator-collector titles had a "4x engagement-time spike" — [Roblox 2021](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2022/01/year-roblox-2021-data)
- Anime games skewed older: All Star Tower Defense, Blox Fruits and Shindo Life each had over 40% of users aged 17+, and Deepwoken over 60%. 17–24-year-olds were the fastest-growing group in 2021 — [Roblox 2021](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2022/01/year-roblox-2021-data) — and again in 2022 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/80671/roblox-recap-reveals-70-new-experiences-surpassed-1-billion-visits-in-2022/)
- Roleplay and avatar games skewed female and young. The 2021 female top 10 was led by Brookhaven, Adopt Me!, Bloxburg, Royale High and MeepCity, and included Livetopia — [Roblox 2021](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2022/01/year-roblox-2021-data)
- 2022 horror: Doors and Evade were called "viral thrillers"; Rainbow Friends arrived July 2022 — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/?p=217919); [List of Roblox games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- Fads tied to pop culture: Squid Game clones (Sept–Oct 2021) and Funky Friday (Feb 2021, riding Friday Night Funkin') — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/squid-game-heads-to-roblox-as-players-recreate-the-shows-deadly-game); [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/2404080894/Funky-Friday)
- Ports of Minecraft formats: BedWars was rebuilt from Hypixel's minigame — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/bedwars-in-2026-can-easy-gg-s-kit-machine-outlast-rivals)

### Inferences
- The genre map split by demographic. Roleplay, pets and obbies served younger and female players. Anime fighting, anime tower defense and hardcore RPGs served older and male players. This split explains why Blox Fruits and Shindo Life grossed highly despite lower visit ranks.
- The horror lineage ran from Piggy (2020) to Rainbow Friends, Doors and Evade (2022), with each wave tied to YouTuber reaction videos.

### Gaps
- No official genre-share percentages were found; Roblox gives rank order only.
- Tower defense as a genre (TDS, ASTD) is not called out separately in Roblox's data.

## What record CCU milestones happened?

### Takeaway
Adopt Me! set the platform's single-game CCU records of the era: 1.6M (Apr 2020), 1.79M (Oct 2020) and 1.92M (Apr 2021). Pet Simulator X later took the 2023 Innovation Award with 1.48M. Blox Fruits' 1M+ and Brookhaven's 1.3M peaks came after 2022.

## How did monetization evolve?

### Takeaway
The era moved from one-time paid access and gamepasses (Bloxburg, Jailbreak) toward recurring gacha-style eggs, pet collection and rarity tiers, and player trading economies (Adopt Me!, Pet Simulator X). Real-money resale and scams followed, as did brand tie-ins, toy licensing and briefly NFTs. By 2021–22 the top grossers were pet, trading and anime-RPG games.

### Cited Findings
- Adopt Me!: Robux-only pets, egg hatching, Neon and Mega-Neon fusion and trading. About $16M+ revenue in the 2020 era, later about US$60M per year. Off-platform resale of US$100–300 per rare pet led to scams and, in Nov 2020, trade licenses and history. ### Inferences
- The egg drop works as a combined engagement and monetization trigger: timed eggs drive both CCU records and Robux spend. This is supported by Adopt Me!'s records landing on egg releases and its player counts tripling during updates.
- In anime RPGs the monetization shifted to gamepasses, rerolls and "fruit" or spec gacha bought by older players, consistent with their 17+ skew and high gross despite lower visits. This is an inference; specific monetization mechanics for Blox Fruits, Shindo Life and ASTD were not sourced.

### Gaps

- Brookhaven's monetization model is not sourced; background knowledge suggests gamepasses such as premium houses and vehicles.

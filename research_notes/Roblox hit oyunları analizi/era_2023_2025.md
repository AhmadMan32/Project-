# Roblox Hit Games of 2023–2025: Catalog, Breakouts, Records, Design Traits, Team Sizes

Research date: 2026-11-08. All numbers come with a source. Roblox does not publish official all-time peak CCU per game, so most peaks come from third-party trackers (Rolimons, RoMonitor, RoWatcher, RoTrends, RoVitals, rblxstat), Guinness, Wikipedia or press. Trackers sample at different intervals and disagree. **Important caveat:** RoVitals and rblxstat "peak" lists only cover peaks *since their tracking began* (mostly Aug 2026). They are NOT lifetime peaks, and for the 2025 games they understate the real peaks by about 10x.

---

## Q1. Catalog: when did each game launch and break out, what triggered it, and how big did it get?

### Takeaway
2023–2025 hits fall into three tiers. (1) 2025 "megahits" that each passed 10M+ CCU, beyond any previous game on any platform: Steal a Brainrot (~25.4–25.8M), Grow a Garden (~22.3M), 99 Nights in the Forest (~14.2M). (2) Million-CCU-class hits: Dead Rails (~1.48M), The Strongest Battlegrounds (~1.4M, Dec 2024), Fisch (possibly ~1.27M, Oct 2025, unverified). (3) 2023–24 hits in the 200K–800K range: Ink Game, The Hunt, Dress to Impress, Rivals, Blade Ball, Pet Simulator 99, Anime Vanguards, Dandy's World, Forsaken, Sol's RNG. The triggers were short-form video (TikTok/YouTube), Roblox's own social channels, live "admin abuse" events, and celebrity/brand collabs.

### Cited Findings — Catalog (one entry per game)

**1. Steal a Brainrot (2025)**
- Developer SpyderSammy (Sammy), owned by DoBig Studios. Idle/stealing game released May 16, 2025. Players buy "Brainrots" (voxel characters from the Italian brainrot memes) from a conveyor belt; they generate income over time and can be stolen from other players' bases (described as capture-the-flag-like). Bases have a temporary shield button, traps/items can be bought with in-game currency or Robux, and "Rebirth" resets progress for better stats. Developers run "admin abuse" events with exclusive characters — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- CCU milestones: ~5M peak and ~7B visits in July 2025; past 20M on Aug 23, 2025 (staged "admin war" with Grow a Garden); 24M on Sep 13, 2025 ("Extinct Event"); 25.4M in Oct 2025, the first game past 25M; average CCU ~1M in Sep 2025; a Bruno Mars virtual concert in Jan 2026 peaked at 12.8M CCU — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- PocketGamer.biz (Oct 7, 2025): 25.4M record; hit 24.1M in September when it overtook Grow a Garden; 30.1B lifetime visits; **average playtime is about one-third of Grow a Garden's** — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players/)
- Exact peak conflict: Guinness lists 25,836,222 on Oct 11, 2025; another tracker cites 25,868,678 (same date); Outlook India reports 25.2M — [RoVitals records (cites Guinness)](https://rovitals.com/records); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-breaks-all-time-player-record)
- Virality: spread through TikTok and YouTube, including viral clips of children reacting to losing Brainrots. Won Best Creative Direction at the 2025 Roblox Innovation Awards. Polygon criticized pay-to-win (the best items are sold only for cash). Epic licensed the concept for "Steal the Brainrot" in Fortnite Creative (July 2025, peak 400–500K CCU). Story Kitchen/DoBig film in development — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Revenue: no official figure. The third-party Profitable.app estimates ~$593M lifetime (model-based, unverified) — [Profitable.app via search](https://profitable.app/tools/roblox-revenue-calculator)

**2. Grow a Garden (2025)**
- Released March 26, 2025 (some trackers say Mar 25). Free-to-play multiplayer idle game, created by anonymous 16-year-old "BMWLux". The first version was built in about 3 days (Jandel). Jandel's Splitting Point Studios bought in in April 2025, when the game had ~1,000 CCU; the original developer keeps 50%; DoBig Studios holds a minority share — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); in an interview Madsen said "three or four days" — [GamesBeat Janzen Madsen interview](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- Loop: plant and harvest crops for Sheckles and buy rarer seeds; **crops grow while you're offline**; seed stock rotates; weekly exclusive items need you online to claim them; pets come from loot boxes; Robux buys speed-ups, perks and **stealing crops** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- CCU timeline (each record tied to an update): ~5M on May 18 ("Blood Moon"; nearly double Blox Fruits' earlier Roblox record) → 11.7M on May 31 ("Bizzy Bees") → 16M+ on June 14 ("Working Bees"; passed Fortnite's 15.3M from Dec 2020) → 21.3M on June 21 ("Summer Update") → 22.3M+ on Aug 23 (admin war with Steal a Brainrot) → record lost to Steal a Brainrot in Sep 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden). Exact peak 22,375,597 on Aug 23, 2025 — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/grow-a-garden-player-count-server-limit-and-record-highs/)
- Roblox officially cites 21.6M CCU and the Guinness "most concurrently played video game" title (June 2025) — [Roblox Newsroom, June 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/06/roblox-infrastructure-supporting-record-breaking-games)
- Fastest Roblox game to 1B visits (33 days); 35.3B+ visits by May 2026; Jandel says ~35% of players are under 13. TD Cowen made bot-activity allegations, later retracted the manipulation claim, but noted rising account creation in the Philippines and Indonesia; Roblox disputed the bot claims — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Events: weekly events are the core driver, with two separate event teams formed in July 2025; a July 26 event featured NFL player Travis Kelce — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Revenue: Calcalist reports $12M of virtual item revenue in May 2025 alone (unverified, no primary source); Jandel has declined to share revenue — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/dj4lxef8r); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden). Profitable.app estimates ~$287M lifetime (model-based) — [Profitable.app](https://profitable.app/tools/roblox-revenue-calculator)
- Sources conflict on 2026 status: one site says Grow a Garden averaged ~19.7K CCU in October 2026 (a big decay) — [ROLearn case study](https://rolearn.dev/case-studies/idle-garden-zero-to-20m-ccu/)

### Inferences
- Timing pattern: 2023–24 hits peaked at 200K–1.4M; in 2025, three games reached 14–26M. The scale of the platform changed: total Roblox peak went from 13.9M (Apr 2025) to 47.4M (Aug 2025).
- Breakouts follow content updates/events (Grow a Garden's records came every week on a Saturday update or event day: May 18, 31, June 14, 21, Aug 23). The "weekly update + Saturday event" rhythm is the main CCU spike mechanism.
- TikTok is the main trigger, through different hooks: Dress to Impress's "Pose 28" emote, Steal a Brainrot's crying-kid clips, Dead Rails' Roblox-official TikTok clip.

## Q2. What were the record CCU milestones (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights) and the platform-level records?

### Takeaway
In 2025, a single Roblox game first passed Fortnite's 15.3M record (Grow a Garden, June 14), then 20M (June 21: 21.3M), then 25M (Steal a Brainrot, Oct 2025: 25.4–25.8M). The platform as a whole reached 47.4M CCU on Aug 23, 2025, passing Steam's ~41M peak.

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden: 5M (May 18) → 11.7M (May 31) → 16M+ (Jun 14) → 21.3M (Jun 21) → 22.3M (Aug 23) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); NYT (Jun 17, 2025): 16.4M, passing Fortnite — [cited in Wikipedia list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- Steal a Brainrot: 5M (Jul) → 20M+ (Aug 23) → 24M/24.1M (Sep 13) → 25.4M (Oct, PocketGamer Oct 7) / 25,836,222 (Guinness, Oct 11, 2025) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players/); [RoVitals](https://rovitals.com/records)
- 99 Nights in the Forest: 14,153,173 (Oct 4, 2025) — [EverybodyWiki snippet](https://en.everybodywiki.com/List_of_Roblox_games_by_peak_concurrent_users); 14.2M — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/with-a-peak-player-count-of-14-2-million-99-nights-in-the-forest-has-an-audience-other-multiplayer-games-would-kill-for-to-find-these-behemoth-playerbases-you-need-to-be-on-a-platform-like-roblox/)
- Platform: record 47.4M CCU in Aug 2025 (Steal a Brainrot vs Grow a Garden competition) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-peak-concurrent-user-count-hits-record-474m-as-steal-a-brainrot-and-grow-a-garden-compete/); 47.3M vs Steam's 41.2M — [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/roblox-sets-record-concurrent-users-gaming-platform/)
- Platform peak doubled from 13.9M (Apr 2025) to 30.6M (Jun 2025); Roblox designed matchmaking for "21M+ users joining a single experience at once" — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/06/roblox-infrastructure-supporting-record-breaking-games)
- Q2 2025 DAU 111.8M (+41% YoY) — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Comparison: before 2025, Pet Simulator X's 1.48M (2023) was among the top marks — [Wikipedia list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)

### Inferences
- The "admin war" of Aug 23, 2025 was a cross-promotional event between two games with shared DoBig ownership. It pushed both games to records on the same day and set the platform record. Coordinated live events are the most effective known CCU lever.
- Steal a Brainrot has higher CCU but one-third of Grow a Garden's session length, so the "record CCU" metric does not equal engagement hours.

### Gaps
- The exact time and CCU of Steal a Brainrot's Oct 2025 peak differ by 25.2/25.4/25.8M across sources; it's unclear which methodology Guinness used.
- Bot-traffic claims (TD Cowen) were retracted; the effect on CCU accuracy can't be measured.

## Q3. What design traits define the 2025 megahits?

### Takeaway
A shared template: (a) passive/idle income (offline growth), (b) rarity tiers + RNG (seeds, Brainrots, pets, mutations), (c) social conflict/stealing (base defense, stealing crops), (d) rotating shop/stock + weekly updates + weather/limited events, (e) live "admin abuse" events with exclusive items, (f) short sessions and a simple mobile-friendly loop, (g) meme IP (Italian brainrot) and short-video virality, (h) pay-to-skip/pay-to-win Robux monetization.

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden: offline crop growth, rotating seed stock, weekly exclusive items that require being online, loot-box pets, Robux speed-ups and crop theft — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Steal a Brainrot: income-generating units, stealing (capture-the-flag), shield/traps, Rebirth multipliers, admin-abuse events — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Contrast: Steal a Brainrot punishes inattention (an unlocked base is an invitation), while Grow a Garden rewards time away; Grow a Garden servers are small (fan/affiliate source, low reliability) — [earnaldo comparison](https://earnaldo.com/blog/grow-a-garden-vs-steal-a-brainrot)

### Inferences
- The "mutations" and "weather events" mechanics clearly exist in Grow a Garden (the update names point to them), but the sources I read don't describe them in detail. They need verifying on the official wiki or in update logs.

- Under 13s dominate the audience, which explains both the meme-IP choice and the heavy monetization criticism.

## Q4. How fast were these games built, and by what team sizes? Monetization and revenue

### Takeaway
2025 megahits started with small teams/individuals and very short build times (Grow a Garden: a 16-year-old, 3 days). After the breakout, larger studios (Splitting Point, DoBig) took a share and scaled them with live-ops teams. In 2023–24 too, solo or teen developers are common (Fisch: solo WoozyNate; Dress to Impress: Gigi started at 14). Official revenue is almost never disclosed. Roblox's DevEx data shows the scale: the top 10 creators average $65.7M a year.

### Cited Findings
- Grow a Garden: first version in about 3 days, an anonymous 16-year-old; Splitting Point bought in at ~1K CCU; two event teams in July 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); "three or four days" — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- Steal a Brainrot: SpyderSammy (an individual developer) + DoBig Studios ownership; build time not stated — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- 99 Nights: a three-person team in New Zealand (Kieft, Angland, Hufton) — [Wikipedia list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/with-a-peak-player-count-of-14-2-million-99-nights-in-the-forest-has-an-audience-other-multiplayer-games-would-kill-for-to-find-these-behemoth-playerbases-you-need-to-be-on-a-platform-like-roblox/)
- Fisch: solo developer WoozyNate — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-woozynate-makes-a-splash-with-fisch/3269481)
- Dress to Impress: Gigi started development at 14 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))
- Dead Rails: RCM Games, a group with ~70 games (a prolific small studio) — [search summary of RoWatcher/Rolimons](https://rowatcher.com/games/7018190066/dead-rails)
- DevEx: $1.5B paid to creators in calendar 2025, top 1,000 averaged $1.3M (+50%) — [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/business/the-top-1-000-roblox-creators-earned-an-average-of-usd1-3-million-last-year/). July 2025–June 2026: $1.7B total; top 10 average $65.7M (~$657M, 39% of the pool); top 100 average $10.8M; median $1,500; 42K+ participants; in Aug 2026, 28% of the top 100 games had launched within the previous 90 days — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/roblox-top-creators-devex-payouts-2026-1819403); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/robloxs-top-creators-average-65-7-million-a-year-but-most-make-far-less-3408675/)

- Revenue claims (unverified): Grow a Garden $12M in May 2025 (Calcalist); third-party lifetime estimates of Steal a Brainrot ~$593M and Grow a Garden ~$287M (Profitable.app, model); BIG Games $100M+ (RoWatcher estimate) — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/dj4lxef8r); [Profitable.app](https://profitable.app/tools/roblox-revenue-calculator); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/inside-big-games-how-pet-simulator-became-a-100m-roblox-empire)
- Dead Rails-type breakouts generate "millions of dollars a month" (unspecified, GameAnalytics) — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)

### Inferences
- Typical 2025 path: a fast MVP built by an individual (days to weeks) → early traction (~1K CCU) → a studio/investor (Splitting Point, DoBig) takes a share → weekly live-ops and event teams → 10M+ CCU. In this path, distribution and live-ops matter more than the build itself.
- DoBig Studios has stakes in Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot and Steal an Egg, making it the period's "hit aggregator" holding company.

- No official revenue figure for any game. Roblox doesn't name its top DevEx creators, so the payouts can't be tied to specific games.

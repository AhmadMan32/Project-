# Roblox Hits of 2026 (Jan – Nov 8, 2026): Catalog, Mechanics, Saturation, Platform Policy

Data-quality note for the report writer: nearly all per-game numbers come from third-party trackers (RoWatcher, Rolimon's, rotrends, GGRecon snapshots, rolearn.dev, Max Power Gaming's Rolimon's-based monthly averages). These trackers disagree a lot, sometimes by 10x. RoWatcher "all-time peak" figures look systematically higher than other sources (e.g. Steal An Egg 14.3M on RoWatcher vs ~9.9M on Rolimon's; Throw Food For Animals 1.6M on RoWatcher vs 28.6K on an earlier rotrends snapshot). Treat RoWatcher peaks as upper bounds. **Monthly average CCU (Max Power Gaming / Rolimon's)** is the most consistent series available and should be preferred for ranking. Clone games share names, so always match the Roblox game ID. "Age range" and "monetization" were rarely reported per game; where they are missing they are listed as gaps, not guessed.

## Q1. What are the top 2026 hits, when did each break out, and what triggered it?

### Takeaway
The biggest 2026-born hit is **Steal An Egg** (launched Jul 25, 2026). It averaged ~543K CCU in August and was ~1.1–1.36M live/average CCU in Sep–Oct. Reported peaks range from ~2M to 14.3M depending on the tracker. Earlier 2026 waves were **+1 Speed Keyboard Escape** (Mar), **Kick a Lucky Block** (Apr), **Animal Hospital (Anomaly)** (May), **Sell Lemons** (May) and **Grow a Garden 2** (Apr–Jun). The autumn wave was mostly Steal An Egg spin-offs and counter-games (Break and Steal an Egg, Throw Food For Animals, Fake an Egg, Climb For Animals Eggs) plus novelty games (DONT SPLASH, RoWorld, Backseat Rally). Most summer breakouts collapsed within 4–8 weeks: Grow a Garden 2 fell from ~479K to ~21K during August.

### Inferences
- 2026 hits came in roughly three waves: (1) Q1 brainrot/anime holdovers and +1 obbies (Escape Tsunami, Sailor Piece, +1 Speed Keyboard Escape); (2) May–July "anomaly horror" and sequels (Animal Hospital, Grow a Garden 2, Kick a Lucky Block); (3) the July–November egg-stealing wave started by Steal An Egg.
- Breakout trigger patterns: a self-explanatory title plus thumbnail that converts from Home tiles, short sessions, and PvP stealing that creates social stakes. Creator-driven triggers (YouTube/TikTok) were not documented in the sources I found.
- Lifecycles are very short. Animal Hospital fell 62% one month after its peak, and Grow a Garden 2 lost ~95% within August. Steal An Egg held for about three months before engagement rolled over.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox CCU figures for any 2026 game; all figures come from trackers.
- No verified launch date for Grow a Garden 2 (April, May or June per different sources) or Sweep The Moon.
- No source broke down age ranges per game. Monetization was documented only for Steal An Egg (paid Speed), +1 Speed Keyboard Escape (codes/Discord), and Cat Battles (coins/crates/cosmetics). Gamepass/dev-product detail for the others was not found.
- No September or October Max Power Gaming monthly roundup was found. The Oct "1.36M avg" for Steal An Egg is a search-snippet claim only.
- The Bank of America and TheStreet data was not directly verified (403).

## Q2. Which mechanics recur?

### Takeaway
The dominant 2026 template is **"steal X → place in base → passive income → upgrade speed/stat → defend from thieves."** This is the Steal a Brainrot formula re-skinned with eggs and animals. It is combined with **egg hatching plus rarity, treadmill/pull-up "training" stats, luck stats, mutations, fusion, and admin-abuse/scheduled update events**. A parallel template is the **"+1 [stat] per step/second" incremental obby**. A third is **co-op "spot the anomaly" horror**.

## Q4. How did 2026 platform policy changes affect what succeeds?

### Takeaway
2026 brought the biggest structural changes to Roblox publishing in years:
- **Roblox Plus** ($4.99/mo, Apr 30) replaced Premium.
- **New publishing requirements** started May 19: age check for 16+ publishing; ID verification + 2FA + Plus/Premium or a 1,000-Robux refundable fee for all-ages publishing; and an evaluation period with 16+ users before a game reaches under-16 users.
- **Roblox Kids (5–8) / Select (9–15) accounts** went global on Jun 16, and chat requires an age check.
- A **42% DevEx bonus** applies to Robux from age-verified US adults (Jun 8).
- **Recommendation changes** weight long-term retention.
- An **Aug 2026 ban on reward-linked media feeds** for Kids/Select catalogs was triggered by Steal An Egg.

Together these add friction for brand-new studios reaching kids, reward games that also appeal to over-16s, and penalize "doomscroll" engagement hacks. They did not stop egg clones from reaching ~1M CCU in the autumn.

### Cited Findings
- **Publishing requirements** (DevForum, posted Apr 13, 2026; updated Apr 17 and May 11; rollout from May 19):
  - Tiers: personal use open to all. 16+/Trusted Friends publishing requires an age check, good standing and an account at least 2 days old. All-ages publishing additionally requires ID verification, 2FA, an active Plus or Premium subscription *or* a one-time refundable 1,000-Robux fee per game, and passing evaluation.
  - Evaluation: games are first trialed with age-checked users over 16, and signals (account age, play history, platform spend) are checked for bots and fake engagement.
  - Roblox states that new games without an existing audience "may take longer to reach users of all ages" and that games aimed purely at younger players may take longer to pass. It recommends promoting to older users.
  - The "Robux Spent" requirement was dropped. ~100,000 existing creators (with at least 100 hours of playtime in the last 30 days) get six free months of Plus. IARC ratings are coming "soon".
  - Source: [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166)
- **Pilot regions** for the tiered system: New Zealand, Netherlands, Australia, Indonesia — [search summary of BloxBot/RoGuides](https://bloxbot.ai/guide/roblox-new-publishing-requirements-2026); [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/age-based-roblox-kids-and-select-accounts-now-globally-available)
- **Roblox Kids / Select global launch, Jun 16, 2026:**
  - Kids is ages 5–8 and Select is ages 9–15; standard accounts require an age check (ID or facial estimation) for 16+.
  - **Users without an age check cannot chat at all.** Chat is limited to similar ages or Trusted Friends.
  - Games need extra review to enter Kids/Select catalogs, and **social hangout and free-form drawing games are excluded**.
  - Roblox Moments clip sharing is not available to Kids/Select, and social links are visible only to verified 16+ users.
  - "Tens of thousands" of games are available, e.g. Driving Empire, Grow a Garden, How to Train Your Dragon.
  - Source: [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/age-based-roblox-kids-and-select-accounts-now-globally-available)

  - Launched Apr 30, 2026 at $4.99/month and replaced new Premium sign-ups.
  - Gives 10% off in-experience purchases, rising to 20% from month three (sources conflict on timing), plus unlimited private servers.
  - Roblox covers the discount, so creators earn the same per sale.
  - Sources: [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/introducing-roblox-plus-subscription); [PiunikaWeb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/05/01/roblox-raises-devex-by-42-us-18-above-players/); [search summary](https://playtoearn.com/news/roblox-plus-launches-april-30-at-499-monthly-replacing-premium-with-discount-first-model)

  - Source: [PiunikaWeb, May 1 2026](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/05/01/roblox-raises-devex-by-42-us-18-above-players/). One guide claimed DevEx was unchanged; not confirmed from an official Roblox page in my search.

### Inferences
- The 16+-first evaluation means new games must first win over teens and adults. That may explain why the autumn hits were viral, simple, PvP and meme-friendly formats that also attract older players, rather than purely kid-targeted games. It also means established studios with existing audiences (ILGames, Jandel) have an advantage.
- The Kids/Select exclusion of social hangouts plus the chat age gate likely push developers toward structured, objective-based loops (steal/collect/obby/co-op horror) rather than open hangouts. Brookhaven still dominates in its existing form.
- The adult-weighted DevEx bonus plus the retention-weighted algorithm push toward games that monetize older players (cosmetics, private servers) and retain beyond the clone fad.
- The feed ban sets a precedent: engagement hacks that resemble social media get removed from kids' catalogs. Developers should expect mechanics to be reviewed for "natural stopping points".

### Gaps
- No official data on how many games failed or were delayed by the evaluation process, or on how long evaluation takes.
- No primary source on a January 2026 global age-verification requirement for chat. The Jun 16 newsroom post confirms that users without an age check can't chat, but the exact start date of that rule was not established in this research.
- I could not confirm the DevEx +42% figure on Roblox's official DevEx page (only on PiunikaWeb); the conflicting claim is unresolved.
- The god-is-a-geek link between the algorithm change and the stock drop is speculative.
- No per-game figures for age ranges or the share of under-16 players were found.
